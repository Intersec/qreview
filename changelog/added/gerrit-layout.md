- **Gerrit-like layout** — set Layout to Gerrit-like in Preferences. The page
  follows the change page of Gerrit. The Change Info, the commit message and
  the relation chain stand at the top. Two tabs come under them: Files and
  Comments. A click on a file opens its diff under its row. The Maximize
  icon, or `u`, folds the change page away. The classic layout stays the
  default.
